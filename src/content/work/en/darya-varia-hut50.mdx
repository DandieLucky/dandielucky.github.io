## Context

A company celebrating its 50th anniversary needed a set of motion assets for the event: an opener, on-screen material, and supporting videos — all reading as one sequence rather than a pile of loose files.

## My role

Part of the motion graphics team: producing assets and editing video according to the visual guide, then applying revisions from the event team.

## Constraints

- The event deadline could not move.
- Assets had to follow the established 50th-anniversary identity.
- Output plays on event screens, so type and movement must read from a distance.

## Process

1. **Preparing base assets** from the existing visual elements and logo.
2. **Building animation on the timeline** with pacing that follows the music and the event sequence.
3. **QC at event screen resolution** to check legibility and safe areas.
4. **Targeted revisions** based on the event team's feedback.
