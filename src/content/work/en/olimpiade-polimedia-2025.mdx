## Context

The Polimedia Olympiad is an annual campus sports event with a lot of information that has to land quickly: registration opening, competition rules, committee structure, day-by-day countdowns, and the winner announcement.

## My role

Social media content designer: setting the event's visual direction, building the system, then executing every published post.

## Constraints

- Information changes fast as the committee finalises details.
- Each design has to read on a phone screen within seconds.
- Several parties must approve before publishing: committee, campus, and sponsors.

## Process

1. **Setting the visual system**: palette, typography, and placement rules for campus and sponsor logos.
2. **Building information hierarchy**: what is the headline, what is detail, what stays small.
3. **Designing the countdown series** as one continuous set rather than unrelated posts.
4. **Preparing size variants** for feed and story.
