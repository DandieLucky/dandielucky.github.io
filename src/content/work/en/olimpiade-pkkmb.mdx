## Context

Two annual campus events with different characters: the Polimedia Olympiad, which is competitive, and PKKMB, the induction week for new students. Both need content that still reads as one visual family with the campus.

## My role

Sole designer for all social media content of both events — setting the visual direction, building templates, and executing every published asset.

## Constraints

- The event schedule moves fast: information changes and content has to follow.
- A single post is needed by several parties at once — committee, participants, and the campus.
- Everything must read on a small screen within seconds.

## Process

1. **Setting the system**: one palette, two type levels, and logo placement rules.
2. **Building reusable templates** for announcements, schedules, and participant highlights.
3. **Preparing size variants** for feed, story, print poster, and event screens.
4. **Handing over ready files plus a short guide** so the publishing team can reuse the templates without breaking the system.
