## Context

The company turns 50 and needs a set of motion assets for the event: opening, screen material, and supporting video — all of it has to read as one piece, not a folder of unrelated files.

## My role

Part of the motion graphics team: producing assets and editing video to the visual guide, then applying revisions from the event team.

## Constraints

- The material plays on large event screens, so small detail is lost anyway.
- Revisions arrive close to the event date.
- Some assets have to follow the company's fixed logo and colours.

## Process

1. **Translating the visual guide** into motion rules: duration, easing, element order.
2. **Building assets in Illustrator**, then animating them.
3. **Editing in Premiere Pro** and matching tempo across segments.
4. **Applying revisions** and exporting final versions to the event screen spec.
