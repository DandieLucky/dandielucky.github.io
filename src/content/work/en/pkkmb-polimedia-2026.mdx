## Context

PKKMB is the front door for new students: a lot of important information in a short window, for an audience that does not know the campus yet.

## My role

Social media content designer for PKKMB Polimedia 2026 — visual direction, templates, and published posts.

## Constraints

- A brand-new audience: campus-internal jargon is not allowed.
- The schedule keeps moving, so countdown content has to be updated fast.
- It still has to read as one visual family with the campus identity.

## Process

1. **Structuring the information menu**: what a new student needs to know before day one.
2. **Designing a day-by-day countdown** as a repeated reminder.
3. **Designing the twibbon and mascot introduction** so new students can take part.
4. **Preparing feed and story variants** from the same system.
