## Context

A series of training and competency assessment sessions needs visual material that explains the programme to prospective participants, plus documentation while the sessions run.

## My role

Part of the creative and documentation team, also supporting the field technical team during the event.

## Constraints

- The material carries official information: the scheme, the organiser, the schedule — meaning may not shift.
- The agenda is dense, so documentation has to be quick and must not disrupt the sessions.
- Institution names and logos come with usage rules.

## Process

1. **Building the material** for the training and assessment programme.
2. **Protecting legibility of official information**: scheme, dates, and organiser.
3. **Documenting the sessions** from opening through the assessment.
4. **Handing over the output** in a format the organiser can use directly.
