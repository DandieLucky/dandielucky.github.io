## Context

Raw material arrives long and unshaped. The job is turning it into vertical video that holds attention from the first second.

## My role

Editing and packaging the content — not creating the footage itself.

## Constraints

- Vertical format: the screen is narrow, so text and subject both have to stay legible.
- Short runtime, so the opening decides whether people stay.
- Pacing has to follow the material, not be forced into one template.

## Process

1. **Selecting material**: dropping anything that adds neither information nor feeling.
2. **Ordering the cut** so the story makes sense within seconds.
3. **Shaping the rhythm** of cuts against the music and the points of attention.
4. **Adding on-screen text** only where needed, sized to read on a phone.
5. **Exporting publish-ready versions** to the platform's needs.
