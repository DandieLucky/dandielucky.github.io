## Context

I wanted proof of end-to-end identity work, not only daily content. So I opened this project: designing a full visual identity for a small business, from brief through to application on social media and packaging.

## My role

Every role: writing the brief, gathering references, designing the logo, defining the colour and type system, then applying it across several media.

## Constraints

- There is no real client yet — this is practice held to a professional standard, and it is labelled clearly as a self-initiated project.
- Work happens around daily content production.

## Process

1. **Writing the brief** and collecting visual references.
2. **Exploring logo directions**, then choosing one to develop.
3. **Defining the system**: colour, typography, spacing rules, and how to use the logo.
4. **Applying it** to social media feed, business cards, and packaging.
