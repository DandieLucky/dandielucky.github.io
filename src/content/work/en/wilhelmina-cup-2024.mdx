## Context

A school tournament being renamed needed a new identity: not just a logo, but a face that works on posters, merchandise, and winner certificates.

## My role

Handling every visual asset myself: logo, mascot, event posters, merchandise design, and the certificate template.

## Constraints

- The identity has to feel like it belongs to the school, not a generic tournament.
- The mascot lives at many sizes: from a large poster down to a small sticker.
- Merchandise design still has to read when printed on fabric.

## Process

1. **Setting the direction**: the new name, the spirit of the event, and colours that represent the school.
2. **Designing the logo** and testing its legibility at small sizes.
3. **Developing the mascot** in several styles, including a line version for colouring activities.
4. **Building posters** for the announcement, registration closing, and a thank-you post for tenants.
5. **Applying the identity to merchandise and winner certificates.**
