## Context

A snack wholesale business needs content that keeps publishing without a creative team of its own. Everything has to come from one person: from the idea to the ready-to-post asset.

## My role

I am the only person behind the content: planning the monthly calendar, designing feed, carousel and story assets, selecting and editing short video material, writing captions, then scheduling the posts.

## Constraints

- Products change with stock, so templates must be reusable without looking repetitive.
- Production time is short: a batch has to be finished in days, not weeks.
- Every post must read as one brand, even when it was made in a different session.

## Process

1. **Setting the visual direction**: a palette, two type levels, and one product-photo style used throughout.
2. **Building templates** for feed, carousel, and story so batch production stays fast.
3. **Selecting short video material** and editing it in CapCut — cuts, on-screen text, and music.
4. **Writing captions** and scheduling posts against the monthly content calendar.
5. **Reviewing** which posts worked, then reusing that pattern in the next batch.
