## Context

Matahari Grosir sells snack supplies to small shops and stalls. Promotion happens every day, but there is no in-house design team — so content has to be produced fast without losing consistency.

## My role

I am the only person behind the content: planning the month, designing feed, carousel and story assets, writing captions, picking short-video material, and scheduling the posts.

## Constraints

- Production time is limited because the business runs every day.
- Product photos are limited in number and do not always meet catalogue standards.
- There was no written brand guideline — consistency had to be built from scratch.

## Process

1. **Building a simple visual system**: a colour palette, two typefaces, and grid templates for feed, carousel and story.
2. **Making a monthly content calendar** that splits posts into categories: product, promo, short education, and moments.
3. **Batch production**: one design session for several posts at once, so daily operations are not interrupted.
4. **A light monthly review**: which posts were saved and shared most often, then used as the reference for the next month.
