## Context

The brand needed product photos it could reuse: for social media content and catalogue material, with colour that stays consistent between sessions.

## My role

Shooting, selecting the results, and retouching until they were ready to publish according to the client's direction.

## Constraints

- Limited space and equipment, so lighting had to be simple but consistent.
- Product colour had to stay accurate — it must not look different from the real item.
- Results from different sessions had to still feel like one set.

## Process

1. **Setting up a simple set** and fixing the light position used for every product.
2. **Shooting several angles** per product so there are options when composing content.
3. **Selecting** the strongest frames and discarding the rest.
4. **Retouching in Lightroom**: colour correction, contrast, cleaning the background and small marks.
