## Context

The brand needs product photos it can reuse: for social content and catalogue material alike, with colour that stays consistent across sessions.

## My role

Shooting, selecting the frames, then processing them to a publish-ready state per the client's direction.

## Constraints

- Limited space and gear, so lighting is kept simple but consistent.
- Product colour has to stay accurate — it may not look different from the real item.
- Frames from different sessions still have to read as one series.

## Process

1. **Setting up a simple set** and fixing the light position for every product.
2. **Shooting several angles** per product so there is a choice when composing content.
3. **Selecting** the strongest frames and dropping the rest.
4. **Processing in Lightroom**: colour correction, contrast, cleaning the background and small marks.
